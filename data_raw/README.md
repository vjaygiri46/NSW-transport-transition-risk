# Raw data

The original ABS, BITRE, and Australian Energy Statistics workbooks are not
redistributed in this repository. Some of those files contain embedded links and
document metadata that are unrelated to the analysis. The processed CSV files needed
to reproduce the reported models and figures are included under `data_processed/`.

The official landing pages and available download URLs are listed in
`data_sources/source_registry.csv`. To rebuild the processed tables from the original
workbooks, download the cited releases into the corresponding subdirectories shown in
that registry. The download script retrieves the current files for rows that provide a
direct URL, but older archived ABS releases may require manual download.

Raw files should retain their original filenames and should not be edited.
